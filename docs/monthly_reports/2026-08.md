## 4. Market expert lens — the RED

### 4a. What `decision_outcomes.jsonl` actually measures

Before the number: the population. Of 300 rows in the window, **295 are `source: "market"`,
`strategy: "market"`, `band: "watch"`** — the market-opportunity scan, 10 BUY calls/day across
**88 distinct symbols** (EBAY, ABNB, ILMN, AEP, XBI, FANG, IYR, XLRE, ROST…). Only **5 of those 88
symbols** appear in the watchlist scanner universe.

This confirms the standing caution that a "memo top-decision hit rate" read off this file is an
**invalid join** — it is not the memo's capital plan and not the scanner's universe. The metric is
reported here under its true name: the **market-opportunity `watch`-band BUY stream**.

### 4b. Defect 1 — weekend rows scored at a guaranteed zero

**90 of 290** resolved rows (**31.0%**) have `price_at_decision == price_at_resolution` exactly.
Every one is `days_elapsed: 1`, and the resolution dates cluster perfectly:

| resolved_at weekday | degenerate | scoreable |
|---|---|---|
| Sun | 40 | 0 |
| Mon | 40 | 0 |
| Sat 2026-07-04 (market closed) | 10 | 0 |
| Tue–Fri, other Sat | 0 | 200 |

Mechanism: decisions are stamped at 09:03 UTC, before the US open, so they carry the previous
close. A Saturday decision resolved Sunday, and a Sunday decision resolved Monday-pre-open, both
re-read **Friday's close**. No trading occurred, so the return is exactly 0.00% — and the hit-rate
counter scores that as a **miss**. Three of every seven resolution days are structurally
unscoreable, and the raw 29.3% hit rate is depressed by roughly 13pp of pure artifact.

### 4c. Defect 2 — the lane is anti-predictive even after correction

Removing all 90 artifact rows does **not** rescue the number:

| Measure | Value | n |
|---|---|---|
| Raw hit (incl. artifacts) | 29.3% | 290 |
| Strict hit (>0), artifacts removed | **42.5%** — CI [35.6, 49.4] | 200 |
| Directional, ±1% neutral band excluded | **39.0%** (53 up / 83 down) | 136 |
| Mean return | **−0.295%/day** | 200 |
| Median return | −0.395%/day | 200 |

Over the same 30 days the parent scanner universe returned **53.3%** 1d hit at **+0.27%** mean
(n=889). The selected `watch`-band BUY set therefore trails its own universe by ~11pp with
non-overlapping confidence intervals, and loses money on the median call.

I tried to discriminate "data bug" from "real selection failure" by joining the two artifacts on
`(date, symbol)` — but the populations barely intersect, yielding only **5 comparable pairs**
(too few to conclude). What those 5 do establish: `price_at_decision` matches `price_at_signal`
**exactly in 5/5 cases**, so the *entry* price basis is sound. The negative result is not an
artifact of a bad entry price.

**Therefore:** the corrected 39.0%/−0.295% stands as the best available read, and it is bad.
This lane feeds watchlist promotion via the Strategy Lab approval path, so it warrants an audit
before anything else is promoted out of it.
