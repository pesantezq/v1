## WS1 — strategy_score decomposition  [AUDIT COMPLETE]

### F1.1 CRITICAL — the leaderboard ranking is not verifiable
`run_strategy_lab.py:210-212` computes `turnover`, `concentration`, `leverage`
and passes them to `score()`; `strategy_score.py:68` returns a full `components`
dict; `_score_tactic` (`run_strategy_lab.py:226`) keeps only `strategy_score`

### F1.2 CRITICAL — missing data is rewarded on the largest penalty term

and is ranked LAST at -1.1565, a 0.67 gap below the next-worst.
=> The lab systematically rewards absence of scrutiny over unfavorable scrutiny.
   The top-ranked tactic outranks the only OOS-validated tactic because it was
   never tested.

## Corrections discovered during implementation

### F5.1 was mis-cited (corrected by the E1 implementer)
The audit placed the ranking-triggered auto-anchor's inertness in
`daily_governance_run.py`. That is wrong. The real path is:
`auto_approval.py:692-704 apply_strategy_candidate()` ->
`strategy_selection.py:203-249 record_auto_strategy_anchor()`, reached via
`run_auto_approval()`'s strategy branch and gated by `run_strategy_gates()`
(auto_approval.py:298-393). What kept it inert was
`collect_auto_approval_candidates()` (`auto_approval.py:1031-1066`) hardcoding
`candidate_type=CANDIDATE_WATCHLIST` at line 1052, plus `run_stage()` passing
`valid_strategy_ids=set()`. Both are incidental, not structural -- which is why a
structural guard was required rather than a config default.
Flag confirmed live: `sim_governance.auto_approval.strategy_enabled: true`,
`strategy_daily_cap: 1`.

### A defect introduced by new code, caught by controller probing
`market_session.is_past_coverage_horizon()` raised `TypeError` when passed a
`datetime` (`market_session.py:122`, comparing `datetime` to `date`). Because
`datetime` subclasses `date`, such a call satisfies the annotation and every static
check, then fails at runtime -- in the one function whose purpose is to warn a
caller they are past the hardcoded holiday horizon (2027-12-24). The module mixed
conventions: `latest_completed_session` / `sessions_between` take datetimes while
`is_trading_day` / `previous_trading_day` / `is_past_coverage_horizon` take dates.
`session_provenance(datetime)` was unaffected. Sent back for normalization at the
entry of every date-taking public function.
