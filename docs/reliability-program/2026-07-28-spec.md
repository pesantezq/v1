# Reliability & Statistical-Validity Program — Implementation Specification

Derived from the 18-workstream audit in `FINDINGS-RUNNING.md`. Every decision below
cites the finding that motivates it. Snapshot 2026-07-28.

## Governing constraints (restated, non-negotiable)

Advisory-only; no broker order path. Production changes stay human-gated;
`is_human_approver()` keeps rejecting all AI markers; AI-authored approvals stay
rejected at write time and ignored at read time. `outputs/latest/decision_plan.json`
remains the decision source of truth; presentation layers stay consumers only.
Strategy Lab stays sandbox-only. No fail-closed behaviour may be weakened. Every
change that could alter operator-visible output ships behind an explicit gate.

## Rollout classes used below

`validation-only` · `artifact-only` · `sandbox-active` · `shadow-mode` ·
`gated-off` · `production-active`

## Decision 1 — Sequence is forced by a persistence dependency

F1.1 and F3.3 establish that neither a sensitivity analysis nor any
multiple-comparison correction is computable from stored data: `components` was
discarded, and only 4 nested/overlapping window scalars are persisted per tactic.

**Therefore WS1a (persist decomposition) is a hard prerequisite for WS1 sensitivity
and all of WS3.** Any plan that puts a correction statistic before instrumentation
is unbuildable. Sequence: persist → measure → correct.

Rejected alternative: compute corrections from the leaderboard as it stands. Not
possible — residuals (0.15–0.51) exceed the rank gaps (0.0976) they would need to
resolve.

## Decision 2 — Fix interpretation before fixing scores

Two candidate first moves existed: (a) correct `overfit=None → 0.0` so scores stop
rewarding untested tactics, or (b) correct the health verdict so GREEN stops
asserting validity it does not have.

**Choose (b) first.** Rationale: (a) changes 25 of 26 scores and therefore the
ranking, which is operator-visible and needs a gate plus a comparison period. (b)
changes no number and no ranking — it changes only the claim made *about* them, and
it is what the brief asks for directly ("no failing OOS tactic plus no credible OOS
test should be AMBER, not GREEN"). Fixing the claim first also means the score fix
lands into a health system already able to describe it honestly.

## Decision 3 — `overfit=None` must not be imputed at all

F1.2: `strategy_score.py:47-52` maps a missing `overfit` to `0.0`, the best-case
value, on the largest penalty weight (0.8). This is not a tuning error; it inverts
the incentive — the lab rewards absence of scrutiny, and the single validated
tactic ranks last at −1.1565 because it was the only one measured.

**Policy: an unavailable component is never imputed.** A tactic missing `overfit`
must be *excluded from overfit-penalised ranking* and labelled, not silently given
the best value. Ranking a measured tactic against 25 unmeasured ones is not a
ranking.

Also required: `walk_forward.py:77-83` produced `overfit=2.009588` against a
documented `[0,1]` ceiling (`strategy_score.py:33`). Either the ceiling or the
producer is wrong; the spec requires the range be enforced at the boundary and the
violation surfaced rather than absorbed.

Rollout: `gated-off`, because it re-ranks. Ship the gate, publish old-vs-new
rankings side by side, let the operator flip.

## Decision 4 — Effective trials, not tactic count, drives the correction

F3.2: the 26 rows are ~8–10 materially independent families (8 `profile_*` are one
parameterised family via `_apply_tilts()`; 6 `shadow_*` are 2 baselines + 4 overlay
variants; crowd is 1 idea × 2). All 26 share one price panel
(`run_strategy_lab.py:262-271`).

**Correct for effective families, not for 26.** Bonferroni over 26 would be both
too harsh (variants are not independent tests) and misleading (it would imply the
26 were independent). Record the family grouping explicitly so the divisor is
auditable.

Method selection, given ~8–10 families over one shared panel and per-fold data
existing for only 1 tactic today: **defer the statistic, ship the instrumentation.**
Deflated Sharpe / SPA / PBO all need per-fold or per-period return series. WS3 is
therefore two changes: (3a) persist per-fold and per-window series for every tactic
during the lab run — `artifact-only`; (3b) compute the correction once 3a has run
often enough to have data — `validation-only` when it lands.

Rejected alternative: implement White's Reality Check now against the 4 stored
nested scalars. Nested overlapping windows are not independent draws; the test would
produce a confident-looking number with no validity.

## Decision 5 — Absence of failure may never produce GREEN

F2.1 (`is False` never matches `None`), F4.1 (GREEN is a bare `else`; RED is gated
on one hardcoded string), F4.2 (`overfit_unknown` exists and is never read),
F18.1 (`quant_watch_probes` fails to GREEN on exception while `artifact_registry`
fails to AMBER).

**Policy: every GREEN dimension must carry a non-empty `evidence` list.** A
dimension with no positive evidence is AMBER by construction, not GREEN by default.
Health error handling fails to AMBER or RED uniformly — never to GREEN. This single
rule kills F2.1, F4.1, F4.2 and F18.1 together.

Nine independent dimensions (per brief): runtime, artifact completeness,
documentation coverage, data admissibility, statistical sufficiency, OOS validity,
ranking credibility, governance compliance, presentation consistency.

Roll-up (fail-closed, explicit):
- any dimension RED → overall RED
- any dimension AMBER → overall AMBER
- overall GREEN requires every dimension GREEN **and** every dimension carrying
  evidence
- a dimension that cannot be evaluated is AMBER, never GREEN
Documentation coverage may be GREEN while statistical sufficiency is AMBER; the
roll-up then reports AMBER. That is the intended outcome, not a regression.

## Decision 6 — Three logs, one guard pattern

F10.1: the approvals log and the revocations ledger each have a dedicated
`*_unreadable` guard consulted before rebuild; `production_application_audit.jsonl`
— the sole source durable membership is reconstructed from — has none. Confirmed by
experiment: corrupting it dropped `durably_live_count` 1→0 with
`overlay_rebuild_skipped` still False.

**Reuse the established rule verbatim** rather than inventing a third mechanism:
absent → readable; ≥1 parseable line → readable (a torn tail costs one record);
present, non-empty, zero parseable lines → unreadable → refuse rebuild via the
existing refusal path, reason surfaced.

## Decision 7 — Concurrency: lock now, append-only later

F11.1, confirmed by experiment: two concurrent approvals for different proposals
collapse to one, both returning `ok: True`.

**Choose an advisory file lock around the read-modify-write, not an append-only
migration, for this change.** Rationale: F12.2 found
`gui_v2/data/dash_governance.py` reimplements the approval fold independently of
`promotion_approvals.effective_approvals`. An append-only migration must update that
duplicate reader or the two will disagree about production authority — a larger and
riskier change than the defect warrants. The lock closes the confirmed data-loss
path now; append-only is recorded as a tracked follow-up with the duplicate-reader
consolidation as its first step.

## Decision 8 — Freshness needs a session concept the repo does not have

F8.2: no artifact records a market-session-relative provenance;
`decision_plan.json:lineage.freshness` is a flat 26-hour wall-clock window with no
calendar awareness; the only calendar code is a private `_NYSE_HOLIDAYS` set inside
`resolution_due_probe.py`, hardcoded through 2027-12-24 and unused elsewhere.

F8.1 is the cheapest high-value fix in the entire program: `coherent_run_ids()`
already exists, is already tested, and was built for exactly the stale-mixing
scenario — it is simply never called.

**Sequence: wire the existing safeguard first, then generalise the calendar.**
(8a) call `coherent_run_ids()` from the daily consumer path — `validation-only`,
reports incoherence without blocking. (8b) promote `_NYSE_HOLIDAYS` to a shared
session helper and add `source_data_through` / `latest_session_represented` to
decision-critical artifacts — `artifact-only`. (8c) enforce a per-consumer maximum
staleness, with a distinct `DATA_BLOCKED_STALE` state — `gated-off` initially.

## Decision 9 — Watchlist removal: the evidence model, not a threshold

The prior gate (`resolved ≥ 30 AND hit_rate < 0.40 AND raw mean < 0`) is not to be
restored. It was wrong on both live candidates in opposite directions: it would have
removed TSLA (week-clustered mean **+0.253**) and spared SMCI (**−0.257**).

Five-state model: `KEEP`, `WATCH`, `REMOVE_CANDIDATE`, `DATA_INSUFFICIENT`,
`DATA_BLOCKED`. `REMOVE_CANDIDATE` requires all of A–E:

- **A admissibility** — `data_mode=="live"`, `degraded_mode==0`, acceptable regime
  and price data quality, valid symbol mapping, freshness vs the latest completed
  session, no look-ahead, no silent zero. Failure → `DATA_BLOCKED` (never KEEP).
- **B sufficiency** — configurable minimums for qualified outcomes, distinct dates,
  distinct ISO weeks, non-overlapping effective observations, regimes where
  feasible, evaluation span. Persist raw count, distinct dates, distinct weeks,
  estimated effective n, overlap structure, and every exclusion with its reason.
  Failure → `DATA_INSUFFICIENT`.
- **C robust expectancy** — a policy-fixed estimator, never chosen per symbol.
  Compare 10% trimmed mean, winsorized mean, Huber, median-of-means, week-clustered
  mean on the synthetic battery, then fix one. Threshold must be a *meaningful*
  negative level with documented units, horizon, benchmark basis, rationale and
  sensitivity — not `< 0`.
- **D uncertainty** — one-sided upper confidence bound below the threshold, using a
  dependence-aware resampling method.
- **E influence** — max leave-one-out robust expectancy still below threshold,
  blocking by observation *and* by week. If one observation or week controls the
  result → `WATCH` or `DATA_INSUFFICIENT`.

Expected behaviour at today's scale (~5 distinct weeks): `DATA_INSUFFICIENT` for
every candidate. **That is the correct answer, not a failure of the gate.**

Diagnostics remain visible and never gate: raw mean, median, trimmed/winsorized
mean, hit rate, worst, best, stdev, distinct dates, distinct weeks, effective n, CI,
leave-one-out, regime breakdown. Mean-and-median sign agreement is documented as an
interim fail-closed guard only — a negative median does not imply negative
expectancy, because positively skewed momentum/breakout profiles legitimately show
many small losses and few large winners.

Rollout: `shadow-mode` first — emit the state and full evidence for every symbol
without acting on it, so the operator can compare against judgement before anything
is gated on it.

## Decision 10 — Overlapping windows: honesty over sophistication

At ~5 effective weeks, a stationary bootstrap's parameters cannot themselves be
estimated reliably. **Primary: week clustering with an explicit effective-sample-size
report. Diagnostic fallback: moving-block bootstrap with a fixed seed.** Tests must
demonstrate that naive daily intervals are more optimistic than dependence-aware
intervals on a synthetic set with known overlap.

## Decision 11 — Make bypasses visible rather than uniform

F15.1: static and fallback watchlists bypass every fundamental screen; the
sim-governance overlay path has no fundamental screen at all (only
evidence-corroboration); `watchlist_source` records where a symbol came from but
never which filters ran. MARA fails two live filters (market cap $4.49–4.62B vs the
$5B floor; price $11.77 below its $12.02 200-DMA).

Per the brief, do **not** force every path through identical rules. Instead record
per symbol: admission path, filters run, filters bypassed, policy reason per bypass,
and whether it would pass the standard screen today. A static symbol below the floor
becomes a **visible policy exception**, not a silently-unscreened holding.
Rollout: `artifact-only`.

## Decision 12 — Concerns must not close by age

F16.1: all three detectors auto-resolve on a 60-day age rule regardless of whether
the concern was fixed, and only one detector can ever escalate to RED.

**Replace age-based auto-resolve with closure evidence.** A concern closes when its
detector no longer fires *and* a closure record exists; otherwise it persists.
Escalation keys on persistence + impact + trust-boundary severity. Trust-boundary
failures (timestamp leakage, revocation resurrection, decision/presentation
divergence) are eligible for immediate RED regardless of age.
